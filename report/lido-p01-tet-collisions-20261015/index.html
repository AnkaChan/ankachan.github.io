P01 tet-surface collisions

Rollouts of trained P01 networks (epoch 20 of the 24-hour regime), drawn on the tet surface that the tet-surface contact detects on, not on the carrier cells. A triangle turns red while one of its vertices owns an active contact row: a floor, static-face or division-plane row with a gap below the contact radius r = 0.5 cell. The videos play at one third of real time. The two asset arms use the first library (vertex occupancy rule); no arm on the overlap library has trained yet. "Penetration" is the rollout diagnostic in units of r.

D1_c05: asset library, division planes, carrier weight 0.05 (epoch 20)

K1_c05: asset library, coupled constraint, carrier weight 0.05 (epoch 20)

D1L: lattice tets, division planes (epoch 20)

K2L: lattice tets, coupled constraint, world edges (epoch 20)
