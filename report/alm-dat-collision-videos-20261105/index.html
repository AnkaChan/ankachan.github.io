ALM-DAT on the collision-fidelity benchmark: headline videos

Recorded 2026-11-05 on the Horde L40 VM. Branch ankac/alm-dat-fable-review at 986b7c921 with the uncommitted ALM-DAT changes of that day (friction, damping, smooth compliant-to-hard transition, end-point motion budget). Every ALM-DAT panel uses the distance budget rule: the committed displacement from the detection pose stays inside a sphere of radius 0.5 x 0.85 x (margin + gap). Page built 2026-11-05.

Each video shows the same case under several configurations, side by side, 1280x720 per panel, 30 frames per second, real time. A panel whose run stopped early holds its last frame behind a red bar that names the stop time and the reason (nonfinite = a non-finite state was observed). Hold outcomes are the benchmark's own criteria: "lost at t" is the first proven hand-object separation.

What the videos show

  • Speed cap. One self-contact detection per substep bounds the committed motion to half the detection reach per substep. For the cloth recipes (3 mm reach at 300 Hz) that is 0.38 m/s. The Franka lift peaks at 0.49 m/s, so every garment slips at the lift. A 3 mm detection gap (5 mm reach) raises the cap to 0.64 m/s and the garments hold, at a steep price in records (table below). The design review proposes a second detection at the certified anchor instead (decision D3), which doubles the cap at the same reach.
  • Metric floor. The ALM row's penalty metric is floored at the contact stiffness. On light payload tetrahedra this makes the multiplier update overshoot and the run goes non-finite within seconds. With the floor off, ALM-DAT completes the bag cases and holds the bags.
  • Soft stacks. ALM-DAT rests quieter than the penalty path with zero confirmed self-crossings.
  • Jacket. The native jacket mesh self-crosses under every solver; that is the mesh, not the contact.
  • Budget rule. The first ALM-DAT revision used a path-length style bound (|offset| + |step| ≤ B) that froze vertices at the cap and sank the cloth. It was removed on 2026-11-05 (decision D1); the end-point rule shown here is now the only behaviour.

The one-detection budget of the ALM-DAT path caps the committed motion at 0.38 m/s for this recipe (0.5 x 0.85 x 3 mm per 1/300 s), so the towel slips out at the lift and lands on the table edge. The 3 mm detection gap raises the cap to 0.64 m/s and the towel is carried like under VBD, at 14 to 19 times the self-contact record demand.
