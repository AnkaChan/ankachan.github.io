NeIO on Newton's examples

Neural Intrinsic Optimizer in place of SolverVBD inside Newton's own examples. 2026-11-25 (Pacific time).

Summary

  • What this is. 33 examples of python -m newton.examples that have a SolverVBD path. Each example runs unchanged; where it constructs SolverVBD, it gets a SolverNeIO. The scene, the time step, the substeps, the controls and the camera are the example's own.
  • Three runs per example. The example as it ships; NeIO with the iteration count of the example; NeIO with 30 iterations.
  • Networks. NeIO rigid uses the checkpoint rigid_e at 270,000 steps (it reads the last three iterations). NeIO deformable uses the cloth checkpoint of stage 1, also for tets.
  • These examples are outside the training of the networks in several ways: the time step (often 1/600 s), the iteration counts (1 to 20), the materials and the masses. The page shows what happens; it is not a tuned result.

How to read the page

  • The scene of an example is not changed. Margins, contact stiffness, friction, materials, masses and the time step are the example's. On the solver side: joints are a plain penalty with the stiffness the example gives SolverVBD (which treats them as hard joints); options that only SolverVBD has are dropped; one normalisation group per world; the cubic contact law has a band length of 5 mm for body-body contact, a default of SolverNeIO.
  • Error left: for every time step, the gradient norm at the final iterate divided by the gradient norm at the first iteration; the median over the steps, in percent. 100 % means no progress.
  • Mean distance to the shipped run: over all frames and all particles (or bodies), in the length unit of the example. Some examples use metres, some centimetres; compare the distance with the scene size given under the example name. The shipped run is not a converged reference, and it uses another contact law, so a distance is not an error. A distance that is large against the scene size means another motion.
  • Time per frame is the wall time of one frame of 1/60 s on a shared GPU. An example that does not capture its step runs NeIO eagerly and is slow.

Runs that reached the end: 29 of 33 with the example's iteration count, 32 of 33 with 30 iterations. A run that reaches the end is not always a correct motion: see the note of each example.
